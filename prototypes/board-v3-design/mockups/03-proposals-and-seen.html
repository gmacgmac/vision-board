03 — Proposals and seen

Two things the board has to show without getting in the way. Part A: the agent proposes changes it read from somewhere (an email pasted into the chat, an attached sheet) and you accept or reject each one, or all at once. Part B: items that moved on since you last looked carry an "N updates" tab, and you step through each unseen version until you are up to date.

Recommendation: proposals A (border tab plus footer row), unseen versions B (the card expands in place). Both keep the change on the card it belongs to and keep the card's content untouched.

Part A — Agent proposals

The example: you pasted Northwind's email into the chat. The agent proposes three changes from it and the finance sheet. A fourth, older proposal is out of date because you edited 45 after it was made, so it cannot be accepted until the agent looks again.

Board level (the same in A, B and C)

A glass bar above the board counts the proposals. Accept all opens a list of exactly what will be applied, and skips the out-of-date one with a reason.

Recommended

The card grows by one section for the version you are on, and its footer becomes the stepper. The board stays visible and the change sits right under the text it changed. When you reach the end, the tab is gone and the card shrinks back.

Option C — Version chips under the card

A row of version chips hangs under the card, unseen ones dotted. Choosing a chip shows that version's changes in a small panel; the chips double as the history. Most compact, but small targets and an extra row on a busy board.
