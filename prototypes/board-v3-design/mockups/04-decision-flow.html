04 — Decision flow

One item's whole history, read from the ledger: what you asked for, the key points, every version with its change chips, your notes, the decision and when it was made, and what the agent did after. The example is 41, "Do we grandfather existing annual plans?", from the request on Monday to the decision on Thursday.

Recommendation: A, the vertical spine. Every step is a real card in the board's own style, the times line up down one edge, and it fits a narrow side panel as well as the full stage.

Option A — Vertical spine of cards

Recommended

A line down the left with the time beside each dot; each step is a full 01b card. Versions carry the item's own colour, your words are neutral cards with the note style, the decision is the filled dot.

Option B — Horizontal lane of compact cards

Time runs left to right on one track, day labels on the dots, each step a small card. Shows the rhythm of the work at a glance and suits a wide stage; long histories scroll sideways (focus the lane and use the arrow keys).

Option C — Conversation column, node pinned on top

The node's current card stays pinned at the top while the history reads as the chat that produced it: your messages and notes on the right, the agent's runs with version and change chips on the left. Most familiar, but the versions are described rather than shown as cards.
