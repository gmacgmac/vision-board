Note242: What drives the onboarding…Selected42
Board changes arrive as tool calls; everything else is plain chat.
  • Every bubble floats above the board with an arrow at its card, in that card's colour (42's note is teal, 47's proposal blue). The card keeps its size and its neighbours stay put.
  • Anything that needs your accept or reject is a tab at the bottom centre of the card: PROPOSAL (pink) or OUT OF DATE (amber outline). The tab is the button that shows and hides the bubble, so you can close it to read the card behind. 44 and 45 are closed; 47 is open.
  • Unseen versions stay bottom right (44's "3 UPDATES"). The number, kind and note pin stay on the top border.
  • When several bubbles are open, the lowest item number sits on top.
  • The history button in 41's footer opens its decision flow (section 4).
