07 — Node sizing: S / M / L tiers and the size control (U7)

Cards today are 248px, or 300px when the agent emphasises one, and grow taller to fit. Here the width comes from three tiers picked by a pure rule from the body, so long cards are wide enough to read; a discreet corner control lets you set a card's size by hand, saved like a pin, with Auto handing it back. Section 1 is the tiers, section 2 the three control options, section 3 the same in the light theme. Hover or Tab through any card: the controls are live.

1 — The three tiers, picked automatically

Widths are chosen so body text (13px, as built) sits near 45-75 characters a line where it matters: M and L. S stays narrow on purpose, because a short card is one to three lines and a wider card would only waste the board. Each caption is measured live from the card's own text.

43Decision

Grandfather annual plans for 12 months, then migrate at renewal

Existing annual customers keep their current price until their first renewal after 1 March, then move to usage pricing with a one-off credit worth two months of their old plan. This keeps the Q4 churn risk low and gives support a year to fix the onboarding confusion behind most usage-tier tickets.

What this means
  • Two price books run side by side until the last grandfathered plan renews
  • Finance signs off the credit amount before the migration email goes out
  • Sales stops quoting the lifetime-deal tier from today
  • Teams over 40 seats are routed to the enterprise floor instead

The billing service already supports a plan override per account, so the first year needs no new code: the nightly job applies the override below and removes it on renewal.

{
  "plan": "annual-2025",
  "override": "grandfathered",
  "until": "first-renewal-after:2027-03-01",
  "credit": { "months": 2, "of": "old-plan" }
}
