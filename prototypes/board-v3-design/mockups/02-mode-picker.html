02 — Mode picker

Every container shows its layout on its top line as a pill, opposite the label. Choosing the pill (click, or Enter or Space when it has focus) opens a picker of the five layouts: Grid, Flow, Lanes, Radial and List. The choice applies to that container only. Arrow keys move between layouts, Enter applies, Esc closes and puts focus back on the pill.

Recommendation: A, the popover of tiles. It has room for a one-line hint per layout, works the same on a 400px container as on a full-width one, and does not move any card.

Closed (the same in A, B and C)

The pill names the current layout. Nothing else on the frame changes.
