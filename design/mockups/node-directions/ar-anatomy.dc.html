Node anatomy

Constants in every variant and theme: the number tag on the top border at the left, the answer badge on the top border at the right, the icon tile on the right. The variants differ only in where Bookmark, Comment, Save for later and History live.
WaitingTickedCrossedBookmarked
A · Quiet footerBookmark, Comment and a More button in one 40 px row inside the node. Always reachable by finger and familiar, but every node gains a row, so it is the least sparse.
B · Side railA 40 px rail outside the node's right edge: bookmark at the top, comments below, More at the foot. The body stays pure and one column is the place to aim for, but every node gets wider and reads more like an app.
C · Corner marksMarks sit on the outline: comments as a tag on the bottom border, the bookmark as a folded corner. The sparsest look and the corner fold reads from afar, but an unset bookmark is only a hairline, so you have to learn where to tap.
