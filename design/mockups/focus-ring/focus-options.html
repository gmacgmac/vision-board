Keyboard focus colour: three options

One focus colour everywhere (host-overridable through --vb-host-focus), shown only after Tab, 2px ring, 2px gap. Each row shows the same three things focused: a node card, a toolbar button and the app card at rest. Lc is measured APCA of the ring against the panel canvas and the card surface (decoration tier is 30).
