Timeline

A container whose nodes carry a timeline date draws them on one line across its top, whatever its layout; its other nodes lay out below the line in the container's own layout. Each dated node sits on its own dot, alternating above and below, ordered by date and evenly spaced (one step for the whole line, not to scale); the year is on the line where it starts and where it turns. The date is the node's header, in the kind's colour (or the tone's).

The four headersThe precision is what the agent wrote. Day and time headers carry no year: the year is on the line. The kind is a small word at the end of the date line; the top edge has no tab and no grip, and the number, the disc, the badge and the notes mark stay where they are.
