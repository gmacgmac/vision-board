Timeline layout

A roadmap, a plan or a history drawn as a container in the timeline layout. The date is each node's header, large and bold, the first thing on it, in the kind's colour (a tone's, when the node has one). Titles are bigger than on the rest of the board, and Markdown that opens with headings reads as entries, each with a bar, a bold name and a line or two of text. One line; each node on its own dot, alternating above and below, evenly spaced; years on the line where they turn.
