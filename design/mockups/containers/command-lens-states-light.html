Containers with open corners

A container drawn as four rules that stop short of its corners: the top rule broken by the title, as today, a bottom rule, and side rules that end before the corners. It reads as a group around rounded nodes, not a box around them. Every state the closed border carried has a place on the rules; only the drop target closes the corners.

The statesEach frame holds the same two nodes. A container cannot sit inside another (the format has no parent container, and the six layouts are flat), so nesting is not drawn.

At restFour rules that stop 28px short of each corner, the frame's own padding: the rules span what the frame holds. The title sits in the top rule's gap, as today. The rail is hidden until the frame is in use (railOnHover).
