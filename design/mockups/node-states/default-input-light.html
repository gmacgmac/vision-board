What the agent sends, and what the board keeps

→→
  • A 24 × 24 box with 2 px clear at each side, drawn at 24 px in the 40 px tile.
  • Lines only, in currentColor: stroke 1.7 at 24 px, round caps and joins; no fill but dots of 2 px or less.
  • No colour, text, image, gradient, filter, script or link; a name in kebab-case, unique on the board.
  • Cleaned when added; one that can't be cleaned is refused with the reason, and the node shows its kind with no tile.
