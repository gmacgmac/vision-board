The switch along the bottom is the answer, one click. A dark plate and a switch mean the node needs you; a node that only tells has a light plate and no switch.

Where each action lives in this skin
  • Bookmark: click the number. Save for later: hold the number (or L).
  • History: the "1 update", "New" or "v3" badge in the plate (or H).
  • Comment: the notes button at the plate's right end (or C). It fills pink when the agent replied.
  • Tick and cross: the switch, one click (or A and X). Pressing the lit half clears it.
  • Move: drag the plate. Right-click anywhere on the module for every action in one list.
